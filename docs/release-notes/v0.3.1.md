Fixes a privacy bug: a player who tried to open someone else's grave was told whose it was, whatever the server's player groups said. Upgrade from 0.3.0 or earlier.

A grave's label already showed its owner's name only to players Posse allows to know it (D231). The chat messages didn't follow that rule. In 0.3.0, right-clicking another player's lantern, or `/grave collect` next to it, answered "This grave belongs to" and the owner's name to anyone. On a server with children, that named a child to players outside their circle.

## What changed

- **Opening a grave that isn't yours.** A player who may know the owner still reads "This grave belongs to Alder." Everyone else reads "This grave isn't yours."
- **Access that has run out.** "Your access to Alder's grave has ended." for a player who may know, and "Your access to this grave has ended." otherwise.
- **Graves shared with you in `/grave list`.** "You can collect Alder's grave #2 at …" for a player who may know, and "You can collect a grave shared with you at …" otherwise.
- **The message when someone shares a grave with you.** It names them only if you may know whose each of those graves is. Otherwise it starts "Someone let you collect".
- **Sharing follows Posse.** With Posse installed, `/grave trust` suggests and accepts only players Posse says the sender can reach. A name it won't accept and a name nobody has both get "You can't share a grave with that player." `/grave untrust` suggests only players already on your graves, and answers a made-up name the way it answers a player who never had access. 0.3.0 suggested every online player and said whether a name had ever been on the server. If Posse fails or stops, nobody new can be added, and players already on one of your graves' lists can still be given access. Without Posse nothing here changes.
- **The sharing message reads from the friend's side.** It ended "until you untrust them"; it now ends "until they stop sharing it".
- The four name messages ask the same question as the label, at the moment the message is sent. With no Posse, a Posse that fails, or a Posse that has stopped, nobody but the owner gets a name.
- The owner's own messages, the label and collecting are unchanged. Admins with `lanterngraves.admin` still see owners' names in `/lanterngraves`, and the server log still records them.

## Upgrading from 0.3.0

Swap in `LanternGraves-0.3.1.jar` with the server stopped. No config changes. Without Posse installed, players who share a grave will now see "Someone" and "a grave shared with you" where 0.3.0 showed a name.

## Tested

The name checks below failed on the 0.3.0 jar and pass on 0.3.1, on throwaway Paper 26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3 servers with bots.

- **New `names` suite (49 checks), with a stand-in for Posse.** A stranger Posse refuses gets no name from a right-click, `/grave collect`, `/grave list`, a grave shared with them or its expiry. A circle member Posse allows gets the name in each. While the stand-in fails, and after it stops, the circle member gets no name. When it stops or starts, the labels change at once, and say "Grave" for as long as it's gone. The owner collects in full each time. A player who isn't an admin gets nothing from `/lanterngraves list` or its suggestions. For sharing: a sender Posse allows is offered and can add only players they can reach, a sender Posse refuses is offered nobody and gets the one plain line for a real player and a made-up name alike, and with Posse failing or stopped nobody is offered and nobody new can be added. On 0.3.0 this suite scored 25/49 on each version.
- **`rules` suite (29 checks), with no Posse installed.** Its refusal checks now expect no name. On 0.3.0 it scored 24/29 on each version with those checks.
- **`basic` suite (20 checks)**, as a check that collecting still works.
- **Unit tests: 46, up from 31.** The four messages with Posse allowing, refusing, missing, failing and too old; who a grave may be shared with under the same five; the friend's wording; the owner always named to themselves; and a check that fails when new code reads an owner's name, so the next message gets the same review.

Not tested: the real Posse plugin with these messages or with sharing (the stand-in answers from scoreboard tags; Posse's own suite covers the label). Sharing with a player who is offline while Posse is installed: Posse can only be asked about players who are online, so an offline player can be added only if they're already on one of your graves' lists. That rule is unit-tested and not bot-tested. The `extras`, `hazards`, `crash`, `mvinv` and `griefprevention` suites weren't rerun, since nothing they cover changed. Not yet seen in a real game client.
