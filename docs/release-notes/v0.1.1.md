**Please upgrade from 0.1.0.** 0.1.0 could lose items. Anything a player held on the cursor, or had in the input slots of a screen open when they died, was dropped on the ground instead of going into the grave, and dropped items despawn after five minutes. That covers the crafting grid (the 2x2 in their inventory, or a crafting table), an anvil, grindstone, smithing table, stonecutter, loom, cartography table, enchanting table, a villager's trade screen and a beacon. Everything else went into the grave as promised. 0.1.1 puts those items in the grave too.

Found by the first test self-audit (2026-11-09), which added a bot test for it. 0.1.0 wasn't installed on any of the owner's servers.

## Changes

- Items on the cursor and in an open work screen's input slots at death go into the grave. They're taken from the player only after the grave file is saved; if saving fails, they drop as in vanilla. Containers that keep their items, such as chests and crafters, aren't touched.
- New tests (`test/extras.mjs`): the cursor and crafting grid, an open anvil, items another plugin keeps on death (they stay with the player), and items another plugin adds to the drops (they go in the grave).
- README: "Nothing drops" now says what it covers, and the two "not tested yet" notes are gone.

Upgrading is a jar swap. Grave files are unchanged.
