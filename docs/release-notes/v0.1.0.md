The first release. When a player dies, their items and XP go into a grave marked by a lantern, locked to them, until they come back for it.

## What it does

- Everything that would drop, and all the XP, goes into the grave. Right-click the lantern and each item goes back to the slot it came from.
- Graves never expire, and only the owner can collect one. `/grave trust <player>` shares one grave or all of them, until revoked or for a time like `2h`.
- Items are kept in a file in the plugin folder, not in the world, so explosions, lava, `/kill` and WorldEdit can't reach them. The lantern comes back within a second if it's damaged.
- After a death in lava or the void, the grave goes on the last solid ground the player stood on.
- Finding it: the death screen says where it is, the lantern glows, the owner sees an outline through walls, the action bar gives the distance and direction within 100 blocks, and the recovery compass (and BlueMapHUD's death marker) points at it.
- Crash-safe: if the server dies partway through a death or a collection, nothing is lost or copied. A damaged grave file is reported and kept, never overwritten.
- With Multiverse-Inventories, a grave pays out only in the inventory group it came from.

## Requirements

- Paper 26.1.2 or later, on Java 25. Not Spigot, not Folia.
- Optional: Multiverse-Inventories 5 (keep its `apply-playerdata-on-join` off, the default), and BlueMapHUD.
- Remove any other grave plugin first.

## Install

Put `LanternGraves-0.1.0.jar` in `plugins/` and restart. `LanternGraves.sha256` has the jar's checksum. Settings and permissions are in the [README](https://github.com/ExtantApe/LanternGraves#readme).

## Tested, and not

Tested with bots on fresh local Paper 26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3 servers, counting every item before and after each test: deaths and collection, full inventories, lava, the void in all three dimensions, explosions and other damage to the lantern, the lock and friend access, Multiverse-Inventories groups, restarts, `kill -9` at many points around a death and a collection, and damaged files. Results: [docs/test-results.md](https://github.com/ExtantApe/LanternGraves/blob/v0.1.0/docs/test-results.md).

Not tested yet:

- **A real game client.** The bots speak 1.21.11 and reach 26.x through ViaVersion and ViaBackwards, so how the lantern looks by day and night, and how collecting feels, are unknown.
- **Real players** on a live server.
- **Bot movement on 26.3**, where the bots are teleported instead of walking. They walk on 26.1.2 and 26.2.
- **Combat-log and land-claim plugins**, and other plugins that change death drops.

This is a 0.x release: expect changes after it has seen real use.
